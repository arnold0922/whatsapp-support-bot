Our story

From island to Rokeby Road

A family with Greek heritage, from the island of Kastellorizo, opened Boucla more than 20 years ago. Today it's a Subiaco favourite for Moroccan-inspired surroundings, a leafy courtyard and Mediterranean breakfast and lunch.
